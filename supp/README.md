# Supplementary tables

The S1–S5 publication workbooks are recorded in `table_code_map.tsv`. S1 contains cohort/sample structure, S2 gene memberships and scoring provenance, S3 ARTEMIS patient-level results, S4 spatial results, and S5 the Seo residual-TNBC clinical extension. Earlier small-module definitions and High–Low results remain explicitly identified as earlier sensitivity content, separate from current main-program definitions and correlation results.

`source_data/SourceData_Seo_*.tsv` contains 48 patient scores, all seven available formal model-coefficient records, 146 mapping records (141 measurable) and one nested-model comparison. No clinical data are added to S3/S4. No supplementary figure is added or renumbered. Figure-panel mapping remains to be supplied when manuscript placement is decided.

The existing `Supplementary_Table_S1_FINAL.xlsx` and `Supplementary_Table_S2_FINAL.xlsx` filenames are compatibility copies of their respective current PUBLICATION workbooks, not separate scientific versions.

Additional cohort/sample inventories, gene-mapping records, robustness/null results and cross-context validation from the earlier extended S1/S2 release are preserved in explicitly named Extended/Cross_context worksheets. These records retain their original estimates and scope rather than being removed when the publication-staging tables are updated.

The earlier submission staging area contains tables, while later standalone supplementary analyses contain additional or changed evidence. An earlier table is not automatically the final table merely because its name includes “PUBLICATION.”

The reference Yan study's nine supplementary workbooks were inspected for organization only. No reference workbook is included or modified.

Before adding tables, confirm manuscript ID, generating script, inputs, numerical consistency with final panels, ownership/redistribution and absence of private information. Internal provenance spreadsheets, figure-selection reports and discarded results belong outside the public repository.
